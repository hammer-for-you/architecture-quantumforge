Hawkmoon commanding the power of the Sword of the Dawn
The Sword of the Dawn is a mystical blade which serves the Runestaff and can only be wielded properly by the Runestaff's true champion. It was claimed by Dorian Hawkmoon as his rightfully. The blade glows with a rosy light and can summon the Legion of the Dawn.
Trivia[]
The Sword of Dawn is mentioned by John Daker in his vision of other manifestations of the Champions and their black swords, so it may be possible that The Sword of Dawn was originally meant to be the black blade, before the Black Jewel retcon.
