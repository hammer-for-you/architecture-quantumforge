Objects of Power are recurring archetypal artifacts which exist in some form in nearly all universes. They can be understood as the separate aspects of the Cosmic Balance.
The Objects of Power include:
The Black Sword
The Holy Grail
The Runestaff
The Horn of Fate
The Chaos Shield
The Actorios Stone
