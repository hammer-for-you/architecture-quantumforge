Yisselda is Count Brass' daughter. Her beauty is impressive.
When Baron Maliadus arrived Kamarg she kept several assignations with him. She found his infamous reputation attractive and cannot believe the stories true, for when he spoke to her in his soft, cultivated voice, she saw a man of gentle temperament, forged to appear grim and ruthless by the conventions of his office and his role in history. Finally she found his real personality when she refused to marry him.
