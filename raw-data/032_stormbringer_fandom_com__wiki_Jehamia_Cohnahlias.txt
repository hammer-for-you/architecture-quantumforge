Jehamia Cohnahlias is the human incarnation of the spirit that dwells within the Runestaff (And therefore the Holy Grail). He appears as a young boy with asian features, dressed in a gown sewn so densely with jewels that the fabric cannot be seen. He is able to transform his body into a stream of golden light.
Connection to Jerry Cornelius[]
The name Jehamia Cohnahlias is extremely similar to Jeremiah Cornelius , an aspect of the Eternal Champion. In the Cornelius story The Tank Trapese, Jerry assasinates a young boy whose description is identical to that of Jehamia.
