Ornithopters are mechanical vehicles which achieve flight by beating their wings like a bird, bat or insect. They are utilized by the Dark Empire of Granbretan for travel and warfare.
In common with all other things crafted by Granbretan, the Ornithopters are ornately sculpted, generelly in the shapes of flying animals such as griffins and condors. They are commonly equipped with Flame Lances.
