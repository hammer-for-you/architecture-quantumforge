Elthur is the capital city of the Bloody Tyranny of Albion. It is described as tall and dark and vast, with insane architecture and baroque, grotesque statuary adorning almost every surface.
The streets are paved with iron and the sky constantly boils with a greenish cloud of pollution and dark sorcery. Cedric Shadowbane has stated that the bizarre and grotesque appearance of the city mirrors the insane minds of her inhabitants. The Bloody Tyranny is ruled by King Septimus from his colossal palace in Elthur.
