The Deranged Deity huddled in his cage
The Deranged Deity was once a man named Stalnikov before the Glowing Pendant drove him mad. He rules from his castle, aptly named The Deranged Deity's Castle, and controls his cult with the power of the Pendant. He was slain by Cedric Shadowbane in revenge for capturing and enslaving Aveline, and to retrieve the Glowing Pendant.
