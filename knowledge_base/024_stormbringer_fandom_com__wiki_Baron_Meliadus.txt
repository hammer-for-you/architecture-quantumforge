Baron Brutus of Kroiden is the Grand Constable of the Order of the Wolf and first chieftain of the armies under the Great King-Emperor Septimus. He is a celebrated general of Albion's Bloody Tyranny.
He travels in his dark carriage.
He is the arch-enemy of Cedric Shadowbane, who has repeatedly escaped the Bloody Tyranny's forces and is responsible for disfiguring the Baron's own face in combat. After slaying King Septimus in a coup, he was ultimately killed by Shadowbane during the final battle between the Beast Men of Albion and the Company of the Arcane Scepter.
