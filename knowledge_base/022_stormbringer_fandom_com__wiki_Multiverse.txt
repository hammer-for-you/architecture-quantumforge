Michael Moorcock Discusses the Metagalaxy and the Immortal Knights
The Metagalaxy, also known as the Million Spheres, comprises all existence. It is every possible world, in every possible timeline. It is an infinity of universes spinning through the Second Ether, and it is the setting of all of Michael Moorcock's stories. The two fundamental forces that create the reality of the Metagalaxy are Principle and Mayhem. Mayhem introduces probability in the Metagalaxy, while Principle structures it, creating reality in all its forms. These two opposing forces are held in check by the Cosmic Equilibrium.
The Metagalaxy exists as an infinity of universes, separated by scale, embedded within the field of Time. Travel through the Metagalaxy is achieved by scaling up or scaling down, i.e. dissipating or condensing matter to transcend dimensional barriers. This can be done by accessing the Moonbeam Roads, or by using a Scaling Ship. Gods are limited in their travel through the Metagalaxy and must often be summoned in order to breach dimensional barriers. These barriers are more often than not held in place by the will of the Equilibrium, and it is not a good idea to break them.
The eternal conflict between Principle and Mayhem rages at every scale of the Metagalaxy, and the Immortal Knight exists in myriad forms throughout the endless worlds in order to enforce the Cosmic Equilibrium.
