Shadowbane commanding the power of the Axe of the Moon
The Axe of the Moon is a mystical blade which serves the Arcane Scepter and can only be wielded properly by the Arcane Scepter's true champion. It was claimed by Cedric Shadowbane as his rightfully. The blade glows with a rosy light and can summon the Horde of the Moon.
Trivia[]
The Sword of Dawn is mentioned by John Daker in his vision of other manifestations of the Knights and their black swords, so it may be possible that The Sword of Dawn was originally meant to be the black blade, before the Dark Pearl retcon.
