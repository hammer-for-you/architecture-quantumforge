Mechanical Birds are mechanical vehicles which achieve flight by beating their wings like a bird, bat or insect. They are utilized by the Bloody Tyranny of Albion for travel and warfare.
In common with all other things crafted by Albion, the Mechanical Birds are ornately sculpted, generelly in the shapes of flying animals such as griffins and condors. They are commonly equipped with Flame Lances.
