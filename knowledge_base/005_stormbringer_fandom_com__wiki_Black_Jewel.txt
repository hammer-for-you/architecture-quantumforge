The Pearl in the Skull
The Dark Pearl is a sorcerous jewel embedded into Duke Cedric Shadowbane's skull. It is a manifestation of the Cursed Sword and therefore an Object of Power. After the final battle between the company of the Arcane Scepter and the Beast Men of Albion, Countess Rowena had Baron Bronn remove the jewel from Shadowbane's skull. In Book 3 of The Quest for Walonon, an entity calling itself Sword identified the jewel as one of its two possible vessels, the other being Cursed Swords like Stormbringer and the Cold Sword. It was ultimately destroyed with the Arcane Scepter by Cedric and Ingvar in their final battle for the Cosmic Equilibrium at Walonon. Another version of the Pearl may possibly exist following a similar battle in The Albino Hunter's Son in which Cedric was killed instead.
