The Arcane Scepter is a manifestation of the Holy Grail and an Object of Power.
It is a short staff carved with mysterious runes, similar to those adorning the Cursed Sword. Duke Cedric Shadowbane's great quests and campaigns were largely centered on gaining control of the Arcane Scepter in order to defeat the Bloody Tyranny of Albion.
History[]
"Those who dare swear by the Arcane Scepter must then benefit or suffer from the consequences of the fixed pattern of destiny they have set in motion.
Several such oaths have been sworn... but none with such vast and terrible results as the mighty oath of vengeance sworn by Baron Maliadus... sworn in that year before Cedric Shadowbane, that aspect of the Immortal Knight, entered into this ancient narrative".
