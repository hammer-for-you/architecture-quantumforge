The Bloody Tyranny of Albion is a sprawling empire ruled by King Septimus from his Throne Globe in Elthur.
In twenty years It has conquered most of Europe in the World of the Bloody Tyranny. Within thirty it expected conquer all Arabia and the countries that surround it, within fifty, it seeks have strength for attacking Asiacommunista. Even it knows there are many places the Arcane Scepter is said to be, in Orkanis.
Their entertainment is to debase all humanity. There is something unnatural about every line of the architecture, every choice of color and carving.
Seehaven[]
Albion took the province of Seehaven in the recent past and hold prisoner the Lord Cedric Shadowbane. Their conquests spread like a plague. A healthy enough plague. They establish political orden in their conquests, but scarcely spiritual or moral. Their cruelty is without precedent. They are insane.
In Seehaven their sport was to crucify every girlchild in the city, make eunuchs of the boys, and have all adults who would save their lives perform lewd displays in the streets. And yet, there is also a sense of great strength, of purpose and intelligence. It is so hard to fathom the psycology of these people, when so much of them is paradox.
