The Equilibrium between Principle and Mayhem
The Cosmic Equilibrium maintains the equilibrium of the Metagalaxy. Without the Equilibrium, the forces of Principle and Mayhem would fly out of equilibrium, and the Metagalaxy would be threatened with extinction.
Too much Mayhem would result in an unformed infinity of raw Chaotic probability. All structure and form would break down, all natural laws would cease to be, and life in the Metagalaxy would dissolve into nothingness.
Too much Principle would be just as disastrous. The ultimate triumph of Principle would snuff out Mayhem forever, and there would be no probability, no new creation, no chance, and no novelty. Everything in the Metagalaxy would stagnate, to the point that reality would cease to create itself, and become a static wasteland of sterile nothingness.
In either case, the result is a formless void. This is why the Equilibrium exists. And it is also why the champion of the Equilibrium, the Immortal Knight, exists. It is even possible the Immortal Knight is himself/herself an aspect of the Equilibrium.
The Cosmic Equilibrium is not a physical object, though it can manifest physically in certain universes. It is said that the Equilibrium can exist as the sum of the Objects of Power.
At times the Equilibrium seems to have a distinct will, and could be said to be analogous to a supreme God. It is also likely that the Equilibrium was actually created by mortals, specifically due to Jack Karaquazian and Sam Oakenhurst playing the Game of Time.
The ultimate destiny of the Equilibrium is to be destroyed, along with all of the gods of Principle and Mayhem. The ultimate destiny of the Immortal Knight is to maintain, destroy, and restore the Equilibrium.
