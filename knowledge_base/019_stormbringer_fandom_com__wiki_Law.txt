The Arrow of Principle
Principle, also known as Singularity, is one of the two fundamental natural forces that make up the Cosmic Equilibrium, along with Mayhem. Like Mayhem, Principle can never be good or evil, as it is beyond such human concepts. However, Principle is often seen as the lighter of the two forces. The sigil of Principle is a single arrow pointing up, symbolizing the one true path of Principle.
Principle and The Equilibrium[]
Principle is a necessary force in the Metagalaxy. Without Principle, there would be no stability or structure in nature, no justice or morality in the worlds. Principle, according to its nature, often upholds the Equilibrium. Mayhem is seen by the denizens of Principle as an aberration that would reduce all form and meaning in the Metagalaxy to chaotic flux, a force of Entropy that seeks to mutate all of reality into an ever-shifting void of random energy. The Equilibrium dictates that Principle and Mayhem must be equal in the multiverse, and since that, in itself, is a law, Principle often contests the forces of Mayhem in order to uphold it. In other universes, such as the Second Ether, the champions of Principle are essentially fascists, battling against the freedom fighters of Mayhem.
The Lords of Principle[]
Principle manifests itself in an unknown number of forms and ideals. Chief among these are the Lords of Principle, also known as the Lords of Light and the White Lords. The Lords of Principle are essentially living embodiments of Principle. They are worshipped as gods in many worlds, and reviled as tyrants in others.
