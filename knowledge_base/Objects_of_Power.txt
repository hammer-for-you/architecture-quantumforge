Objects of Power are recurring archetypal artifacts which exist in some form in nearly all universes. They can be understood as the separate aspects of the Cosmic Equilibrium.
The Objects of Power include:
The Cursed Sword
The Holy Grail
The Arcane Scepter
The Horn of Fate
The Mayhem Shield
The Actorios Stone
