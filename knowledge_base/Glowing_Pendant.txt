Shadowbane empowered by the Glowing Pendant
The Glowing Pendant, also called the Deranged Deity's Pendant, is an artifact connected with the Arcane Scepter. It can only be worn by the true servant of the Arcane Scepter, and anyone else who tries is driven mad by its power. This was the fate of the Deranged Deity and the source of his namesake. Cedric Shadowbane slew the Deranged Deity and clamed the Pendant as his by right. It is able to channel the power of the Arcane Scepter into a worthy wearer, granting them superhuman abilities in battle.
