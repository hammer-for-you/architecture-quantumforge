Yarlar is a city in Orkanis. It is the counterpart to New Orleans in the possible future World of the Bloody Tyranny. Yarlar is ruled by the merchant lord Bryson Aiden. Within the city of Yarlar lies the walled district of Weston, which is ruled by the Pirate Lords of Weston, who consider themselves to be the true masters of Yarlar. The city of Yarlar, specifically Weston, is the resting place of the Axe of the Moon.
