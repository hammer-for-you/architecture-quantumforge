Hegoth is a region in the World of the Bloody Tyranny's France. It is the possible future version of the real world Camargue . Herzog Steel is the protector of Hegoth, and he oversees his duty from his fortress of Steel Fortress.
Hegoth is a marshy wetland famous for it's wildlife. Native fauna include White Bulls, Hegoth Flamingos, and Horned Horses.
History[]
Before Herzog Steel, Hegoth was managed by an evil Bulgar sorcerer.
Notable places[]
Steel Fortress.
The Ruin. Over a small hill there is a gothic church which their thick stonewalls have been softened by winds and rain effects. Most part of it was covered by a floral ivy, its purple and ambarine colours covered dark windows.
Hegoth is surrounded on it's borders by a network of towers equipped with various ancient and arcane weapons, able to withstand even the might of the Bloody Tyranny of Albion.
