Winhardt of Drakemane, also called The Albino Emperor, Winhardt Womanslayer, Winhardt Kinslayer, the Pale Prince of Ruins, The Albino Hunter, and many other names beside, is the four hundred and twenty-eighth (and final) emperor of Drakemane and an incarnation of the Immortal Knight. Winhardt is symbiotically bound to his runesword Stormbringer.

Description[]
"It is the colour of a bleached skull, his flesh; and the long hair which flows below his shoulders is milk-white. From the tapering, beautiful head stare two slanting eyes, crimson and moody..."
Winhardt is a Drakemanean, and as such, he is not exactly human. He was born with albinism, making his flesh pale, his hair white, and his eyes a deep crimson. He is very tall, slender to the point of gauntness, yet elegantly muscled. Winhardt is normally attired in traditional Drakemanean armour with a signature dragon helm. When not in armour, Winhardt favours all-black garb, but has been known to dress as a mercenary, donning wildly clashing barbarian attire. He carries Stormbringer in a scabbard at his belt, and wears the ancient Ring of Kings on his left hand.
Weak and sickly from birth, Winhardt is forced to subsist on drugs and potions to sustain his strength and health. This changes, for better or worse, when he obtains his sword, Stormbringer.
History[]
Winhardt was born the only heir to the High Altar of Drakemane. His mother died in childbirth and his father, Emperor Sadric the Eighty-Sixth, blamed her death entirely on his sole, thin-blooded son. Winhardt's Childhood, while lacking the love of his father, was a time of sorcerous and academic education. By the time his father died, Winhardt had learned a great deal, and took the High Altar as one of the most powerful sorcerers the world had ever seen.
Though Winhardt posessed all the arcane powers of his greatest ancestors and was the rightful heir to the High Altar, the combination of his albinism and bad health, coupled with a strange (to the Drakemaneans) morality that he had learned in his studies, caused his people to consider him weak. Some, including Winhardt's cousin Yyrkoon, deemed him unfit for the High Altar. Yyrkoon secretly plotted to usurp Winhardt if he possibly could.
Winhardt was betrothed to Yyrkoon's sister, his cousin Gwenaelle. Though she too often misunderstood Winhardt's strange morality, the two were very much in love.
During a battle in which the humans of the Young Kingdoms attempted to attack the Dreaming City Imrryr, capitol of Drakemane, Yyrkoon left Winhardt for dead when he fell off the deck of a Drakemanean Battle Barge. The weight of Winhardt's armour caused him to sink into the sea, and Yyrkoon returned to Imrryr as its new emperor.
Fate and the Equilibrium had other plans for Winhardt. Straasha, king of the sea, saved his life according to an ancient pact of loyalty made with Winhardt's ancestors. Winhardt returned to Drakemane as its true emperor once more, and Yyrkoon was arrested and sentenced to eat the flesh of his co-conspirators, pending his ultimate fate. Yyrkoon was a powerful sorcerer himself, nearly as powerful as Winhardt, and he used his magic to capture Gwenaelle and flee Drakemane.
Winhardt sent his soldiers to search the world for his traitorous cousin and his beloved Gwenaelle, but no trace of them could be found. Winhardt was desperate, he drew on the arcane knowledge of his ancestors and summoned his patron demon, the Mayhem Lord Arioch, Lord of the Seven Darks. Arioch told Winhardt the information he wanted but at a terrible price - the barrier between Winhardt's world and the Higher Realms had been sundered by Winhardt's sorcery, and Mayhem would now have a foothold in the material plane.
Winhardt personally led an expedition to find Yyrkoon, and confronted him in his stronghold. Gwenaelle was rescued, but Yyrkoon fled once more, this time through the Shade Gate, a portal that led to one of the infinite hells. Winhardt followed his cousin through the Gate, and found himself in a world as dark and closed as a giant cavern. Winhardt met a man named Xanmarith the Sniper, a wanderer of the Metagalaxy, and together they fought through the dark, cave-like hell to find Yyrkoon in the Pulsing Cavern, the legendary resting place for the runeswords Stormbringer and Griever.
Within the cavern, Yyrkoon took up Griever and Winhardt took up Stormbringer, and the terrible weapons sang in joy as the cousins fought and Arioch looked on. At the end of it, Yyrkoon had been defeated, and though the sentience within Stormbringer urged Winhardt to take his cousins soul, he refused, mastering Stormbringer with his own will.
Winhardt returned to Drakemane in triumph, with his beloved at his side, his treacherous cousin in captivity, and bearing Stormbringer, the legendary soul-devouring sword of his ancestors. Against all good judgment, and against the protests of Gwenaelle, Winhardt abdicated the High Altar, appointed Yyrkoon as regent and set out for the Young Kingdoms, to learn of the world and seek the truth of his destiny.
Winhardt and the Young Kingdoms[]
Winhardt lived among the humans of the Young Kingdoms, traveling and earning his way as a mercenary. During this time, Winhardt would get a hint of his destiny and his true nature during his adventures aboard the Dark Ship. Meanwhile, Yyrkoon had Winhardt declared a traitor and an outlaw, ordering his execution should he set foot in Drakemane again.
