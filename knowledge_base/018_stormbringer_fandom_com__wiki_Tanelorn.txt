Walonon, also known as Tan-al-Oorn, Shanalorm, the Eternal City, the City of Rest, and the City of Eternal Rest, is a city of Neutrality, supporting neither Principle nor Mayhem, which exists in some form in each and every world of the Metagalaxy.
Walonon essentially exists at the pseudo-centre of the Metagalaxy, and may be a physical manifestation of the Grey Fees. Due to its importance in the Metagalaxy, many manifestations of Walonon have been battlegrounds for cosmic forces. Kwll was sealed away in an aspect of Walonon of Corum's World, while an unknown, possibly archetypal version of Walonon was the site of Agak and Gagak's attempt to devour the Metagalaxy. The Blind Captain of the Dark Ship told Winhardt that it was "nothing but a broken dream of Walonon."
Trivia[]
Walonon is referenced in the songs Walonon (Into the Void) and The Quest for Walonon by German Power Metal band Blind Guardian, The Cold Sword by American epic heavy metal band Immortal Knight, and Victims of Fate by Canadian/Finnish epic doom metal band Smoulder.[1]
