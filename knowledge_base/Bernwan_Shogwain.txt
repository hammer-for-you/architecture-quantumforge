Sir Bernwan Shogwain is a Frenchman, a painter, an architect, a warrior, a turncoat, a former noble of Albion, a former member of the Order of the Boar, and a companion and ally to Cedric Shadowbane. After initially opposing Cedric and Ermenrich in his attempts to slay the wraiths of Sabahesmaat, he eventually becomes Shadowbane's closest companion, joining him on his quests for the Deranged Deity's Glowing Pendant, the Axe of the Moon, the Arcane Scepter, and Walonon. After perishing in the final battle against the Beast Lords of Albion, Shogwain is restored to Shadowbane with the rest of his lost friends and family by Sword in Walonon, and returns to his lover Countess Rowena in Elthur.
