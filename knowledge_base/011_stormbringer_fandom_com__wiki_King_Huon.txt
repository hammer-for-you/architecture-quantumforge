King Septimus, also called Septimus the Immortal and the King-Emperor, is the undying ruler of the Bloody Tyranny of Albion. He is an ancient, twisted homonculus suspended in a globe of milky amniotic fluid.
In appearance, Septimus resembles a deformed foetus with an enlarged head. He speaks with an artificial voice which sounds like that of a healthy young man. Septimus's limbs are atrophied and useless, and therefore in order to control his Throne Globe, he uses his long, prehensile tongue, which has been described as "insect-like".
King Septimus is well over 2,000 years old, and yearns to extend his life no matter the cost. He rules his empire from his Throne Globe in Elthur.
