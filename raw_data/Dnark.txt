Dnark is a city in northeastern Amarehk. It is located on Manhattan Island and bears a simmilar name to New York City, it's possible past incarnation. Dnark is a city of glowing golden spires and domes, inhabited by living, luminous shadows called the Great Good Ones. Dnark is also the true resting place of the Runestaff.
