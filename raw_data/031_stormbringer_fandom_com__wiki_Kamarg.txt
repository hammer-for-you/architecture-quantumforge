Kamarg is a region in the World of the Dark Empire's France. It is the possible future version of the real world Camargue . Count Brass is the protector of Kamarg, and he oversees his duty from his fortress of Castle Brass.
Kamarg is a marshy wetland famous for it's wildlife. Native fauna include White Bulls, Kamarg Flamingos, and Horned Horses.
History[]
Before Count Brass, Kamarg was managed by an evil Bulgar sorcerer.
Notable places[]
Castle Brass.
The Ruin. Over a small hill there is a gothic church which their thick stonewalls have been softened by winds and rain effects. Most part of it was covered by a floral ivy, its purple and ambarine colours covered dark windows.
Kamarg is surrounded on it's borders by a network of towers equipped with various ancient and arcane weapons, able to withstand even the might of the Dark Empire of Granbretan.
