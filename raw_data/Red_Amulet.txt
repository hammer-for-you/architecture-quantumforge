Hawkmoon empowered by the Red Amulet
The Red Amulet, also called the Mad God's Amulet, is an artifact connected with the Runestaff. It can only be worn by the true servant of the Runestaff, and anyone else who tries is driven mad by its power. This was the fate of the Mad God and the source of his namesake. Dorian Hawkmoon slew the Mad God and clamed the Amulet as his by right. It is able to channel the power of the Runestaff into a worthy wearer, granting them superhuman abilities in battle.
